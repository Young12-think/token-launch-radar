How the number is built

Named peers behind the multiple

Peers are tokenised protocols from the same pool the multiple came from, closest in TVL first. Their multiples are their own market-cap/TVL; the row uses the pool median.

Farm plan — concrete steps, costs, time

    Steps are built from this protocol's own record (category, chains, TVL tier). Costs are gas estimates for the chain named; durations are the plan's own cadence, not a promise of a launch date.

    Set aside — not ranked, and why

    These protocols record no token, but their structure makes a launch meaningless or near-impossible: bridges move other chains' assets, liquid-staking tokens are receipts for tokens that already trade, and centralised exchanges do not airdrop. Ranking them on raw TVL would put wrapped Bitcoin at the top of a launch radar. They are listed here with the reason, not silently cut.

    How the value is scored

    The formula

    Implied value = TVL × the median market-cap/TVL multiple of tokenised peers in the same category. Where a category has fewer than 3 peers, the multiple falls back to its category group, then to the whole field. The result is adjusted for 7-day TVL momentum (clamped −50% to +100%), seasoning since DefiLlama first tracked the protocol, and chain breadth. Bear and bull use the 25th and 75th peer multiples instead of the median.

    What is not in the number

    The snapshot carries no fees, revenue, funding, team or points-program data — so none of those are in the score, and no farm plan claims a points program exists. Multiples use circulating market cap, not fully-diluted value, so implied values are a floor: a token's FDV at launch is usually higher. Every multiple, peer count and adjustment is shown per protocol in its detail view.
