How the number is built

    Farm economics — what the plan costs, never what it earns

    Set aside — not ranked, and why

    These protocols record no token, but their structure makes a launch meaningless or near-impossible: bridges move other chains' assets, liquid-staking tokens are receipts for tokens that already trade, and centralised exchanges do not airdrop. Ranking them on raw TVL would put wrapped Bitcoin at the top of a launch radar. They are listed here with the reason, not silently cut.

    How the value is scored

    The formula

    Implied value = the size curve: log(market cap) = a + b·log(TVL), fitted by least squares on the snapshot's 92 launchable tokenised peers — the peers passing the house exclusion rule, so the curve describes the population the board actually ranks — residuals winsorised at 2σ and refit once, times a full-strength 7-day momentum factor (0.75x at −50% to 1.5x at +100%). The slope is 0.41, so the implied multiple falls as TVL grows. The category-median multiple is still shown per row as a cross-check; it lost the held-out test, so it prices nothing.

    Tested, not asserted

    Every term was put to two held-out tests on the snapshot itself — leave-one-out over the 92 launchable peers, and walk-forward (fitted on peers listed before 2025, tested on peers listed after) — with a cross-check on all 128 priced peers beside it, and the v2 estimator scored on both populations so the gain decomposes into the population change and the estimator change. The size curve won both against the v1 category-median multiple; seasoning and chain breadth failed both and were dropped (re-tested on the v3 base and still failing); momentum survived at full strength. The bear–bull band is the 25th–75th percentile of the model's own leave-one-out errors on the launchable peers. Full numbers are in the integrity strip above and in each row's detail view.

    What is not in the number

    The snapshot carries no fees, revenue, funding, team or points-program data — so none of those are in the score, and no farm plan claims a points program exists. Multiples use circulating market cap, not fully-diluted value, so implied values are a floor: a token's FDV at launch is usually higher. Farm verdicts price gas cost against the position size only; no return is ever claimed. Every curve value, cross-check and adjustment is shown per protocol in its detail view.
